# Product

**Working name:** Carbon-Aware Supply Chain Dashboard  
**Users:** sustainability teams, supply-chain managers, ESG auditors, suppliers (later).

## Problem

Companies cannot see Scope 3 (supplier) emissions across tiers. They need an auditable footprint, hotspots, and circular alternatives (materials, energy, transport, local sourcing).

## What we are building

1. Ingest supplier activity (CSV, manual, demo).
2. Calculate CO₂e per supplier with documented factors.
3. Show totals, ranking, hierarchy, breakdown, map.
4. Recommend alternatives with estimated ΔCO₂e from the **same engine**.
5. What-if sliders. Later: trends, ESG PDF, chatbot.

## Out of scope for v1

- Live supplier APIs / ERP connectors
- Real regulatory filing
- Multi-org SaaS billing
- ML that **is** the official inventory number
